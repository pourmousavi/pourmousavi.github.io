Before You Contact Me

I receive a large number of emails from prospective students. Adelaide University has changed how research degree scholarships work, and there are now only three ways into a funded research degree in my group.

  • A funded project position. When I hold PhD or postdoc funding from a grant (ARC Linkage, ARC Discovery, industry or other sources), I recruit openly under the University's rules, including international candidates. These positions are always advertised publicly - on the Adelaide University website, on my LinkedIn page, and on other relevant platforms.
  • A Signature Research Theme (SRT) scholarship round. If I win a scholarship through an SRT Project Round (up to twice a year), I can recruit any qualified student, international candidates included. Successful projects are advertised on the University's research projects page and announced on my LinkedIn.
  • The Graduate Research Talent Scheme. Three rounds a year, open to domestic applicants and to international applicants who have completed a degree at an Australian or New Zealand university. This is the only pathway where contacting me before an advertisement appears is useful. See the sample projects below for the kind of topic to propose.

If the Graduate Research Talent Scheme does not apply to you, there is nothing for me to assess in advance - the openings simply do not exist until they are announced. Following my LinkedIn and the University's scholarship page is the fastest way to hear about them.

Watch for Announced Positions

This is not a judgement on your record. Under the current rules, an international applicant without an Australian or New Zealand qualification can only be funded through an advertised project - a funded project position, or a project from an SRT scholarship round - and those openings exist only when the funding does.

What to do

  • Follow my LinkedIn page, where I announce every opening in my group
  • Watch the University's scholarship page and research projects page for SRT round projects across the whole University
  • Check whether your own government offers a scholarship for study abroad - if you hold one, see the "Something else" option in this checker
  • Meet the English language requirements before an opening appears, so you are ready to apply

I am not able to reply to speculative enquiries when no position is open, so please do not send a CV until something is advertised.

Build Your Record First

Scholarship rounds are highly competitive and are decided largely on academic record. Below a GPA of 6.0 out of 7.0, or without a strong research project behind you, an application is unlikely to succeed.

The Master of Research pathway

The Master of Research is a 1.5-year degree that combines research training with an independent project. It is a realistic way to build the record a PhD scholarship needs, and strong performance can lead into a PhD. Tuition is free for domestic students; international students pay fees, even if their earlier degree was completed in Australia.

Other ways to strengthen an application

  • Publish your capstone or thesis work, ideally in a Q1 journal
  • Gain research experience as a research assistant or in industry R&D

If your interests line up with our research areas and you would like to discuss the MRes route, you are welcome to email me.

ARC DECRA Fellowship

If you are an early-career researcher with an outstanding track record and want to apply for an ARC DECRA with me as your host, you are welcome to contact me directly. The scheme is extremely competitive, so you should have:

  • 10+ first-author Q1 journal papers
  • A compelling research idea that fits our areas

Government-Funded Research Degree

I may support your admission application if:

  • Your research aligns with our projects
  • You hold confirmed funding from your government for the full duration of the degree
  • Your GPA is 75% or above

PhD Student Seeking a Sabbatical or Visiting Stay

I may host you if:

  • Your research aligns with our projects
  • You have financial support from your institution
  • You have published at least 1 Q1 journal paper

Our Research Areas

Your research experience should be relevant to at least one of these areas - either by subject matter or by methods/techniques you use (e.g., optimization, machine learning).

  • Battery energy storage systems: optimal sizing, operation, and aggregation for grid applications
  • Electric vehicle integration: charging optimization, V2G, and fleet management
  • Mining/industrial electrification: battery-electric vehicles and hybrid renewable systems
  • Demand response and flexibility aggregation: VPP operation and prosumer coordination
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning for power systems forecasting and optimization
  • Electricity market participation: bidding strategies and revenue optimization
  • Community batteries and local energy markets: sizing, operation, and business models
  • Smart grid technologies: large-scale data analytics, data compression, and prosumer privacy

Also check our recent publications to get a better picture of ongoing research.

Why Join Our Research Group?

  • Work on cutting-edge projects with tangible real-world impact in renewable energy and electrification
  • Access to state-of-the-art HPC facilities and industry partnerships with major organizations
  • Opportunities for industry placements and international collaborations
  • Strong track record of research impact by conventional and non-conventional metrics
  • Pathway to commercialization through spin-offs like OptiGrid Pty Ltd
  • Supportive environment with proven supervision track record
  • Competitive funding packages with the possibility of top-up scholarships and research allowances

What We Offer

  • Competitive Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) scholarships
  • Top-up scholarships from industry partners (up to $10K p.a. additional funding)
  • Research allowances for conferences, equipment, and travel ($5K+ p.a.)
  • Industry-PhD programs with additional benefits and guaranteed industry placements
  • International placement opportunities (e.g., 6-month placements in Denmark with flights and stipend)
  • Access to Adelaide Power System Summer School intensive training
  • Collaborative research environment with regular group meetings and workshops
  • Opportunities to co-author papers with international collaborators
